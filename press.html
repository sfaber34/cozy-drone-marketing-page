Elevator pitch

Cozy Drone is a satirical drone-warfare game disguised as a cozy experience. Fly a fixed-wing military drone over a cheerful pixel world, surveil the locals to find the bad guys, and hand out freedom by laser-guided missile. You can't crash, you can't run out of ammo, and you cannot lose.

Full description

In Cozy Drone you command a next-generation remotely piloted aircraft flying over a colorful pixel world chock full of happy, smiling folks. Surveil the locals to work out who's an average citizen going about their day and who's the bad guy. Eliminate the bad guys with laser-guided missiles and high-explosive rounds. It's a cozy game through and through. You can't crash. You can't run out of ammo or fuel. You're never penalized for hitting the wrong target. You simply cannot lose. It's just like all those cute cozy farming sims, except instead of harvesting crops you're harvesting souls. The full game is about 20 minutes of retro-pixel satire. It's an ode to the simpler era of pointless indie flash games.
