Welcome to the future of ethical warfare, Airman

You've been granted the privilege of commanding a next-generation remotely piloted fixed-wing aircraft, equally suited to Intelligence, Surveillance and Reconnaissance and to precision strike.

Fly over a colorful pixel world chock full of happy, smiling folks. Surveil the locals to work out who's an average citizen going about their day and who's the bad guy. Then use laser-guided missiles or high-explosive rounds to take them out.

Cozy Drone is a cozy game through and through. You can't crash. You can't run out of ammo or fuel. You don't get penalized for hitting the wrong targets. You simply cannot lose — it's just like all those cute cozy farming sims, except instead of harvesting crops you're harvesting souls.
